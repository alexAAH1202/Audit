# LEAVE section — DRAFT for consultant review

Status: **draft, not in the tool.** Nothing here has been added to `access-audit.html`.
Every question below needs sign-off from an accredited access consultant before it
goes into client work (design brief, Phase 2: "A LEAVE section exists with at least six questions").

## The honest starting point

Emergency egress for people with disability is **not prescribed** by the Premises
Standards, and the NCC has little that is specific to it. The ABCB's own work records
emergency egress as a matter the Standards could not include, and it ran a separate
"egress for all occupants" project and a 2015 Regulation Impact Statement on it.
So most of this section is **best practice against recognised guidance**, not
"Breach of Code" territory. That matters for the tool: `Breach of Code` should be
rare to unavailable in this section, and the report must not present a best-practice
gap as a legal failure.

Consequences for how I drafted it:

- Only questions that rest on a provision I could actually find are placed in
  **Legislative Requirements**, and even those are marked for you to confirm.
- Where I could not confirm a clause, the Basis column says **"clause TBC"** rather
  than guessing a number. Please do not accept a clause reference from this draft
  without checking it against the edition the template uses.
- Clause references to **AS 1428.1** are copied from the template's existing pages
  (stairs Cl 11, handrails Cl 12, doors Cl 13, TGSIs per AS 1428.4.1). I have not
  re-checked those against the standard; I reused them for consistency.

## Page 2 — Refuge & Assisted Evacuation

This page is **entirely best practice**: I could not find an NCC requirement to provide refuge
areas in general, and the sources I read treat them as good practice within the
building's emergency plan.

### Best Practice Recommendations

| # | Question | Basis | Conf. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | A refuge area or other safe waiting area is provided, or a documented alternative exists, for people who cannot use stairs | AS 3745 / ABCB guidance, clause TBC | C |
| 17 | Each refuge area is within or beside a fire-isolated exit and does not obstruct the exit width | Best practice. Clause TBC | C |
| 18 | Each refuge area has clear floor space of at least 800 x 1300mm per wheelchair space | Best practice. Dimension TBC | C |
| 19 | Each refuge area is signed, and the sign is tactile or high-contrast and readable from the approach | Best practice. Clause TBC | C |
| 20 | Each refuge area has a way to call for help that works for deaf and hearing-impaired people as well as speakers | Best practice. Clause TBC | C |
| 21 | Staff or wardens know where refuge areas are and have a documented way to help a person from them | AS 3745, clause TBC | C |
| 22 | Where an evacuation device or lift is intended for assisted evacuation, it is tested and its use is in the emergency plan | ABCB lift-evacuation guidance, clause TBC | C |

## Open questions for you

1. **Group placement.** Which questions, if any, do you want treated as Legislative? My
   position is that only #1–#4 have a code basis, and #4 is doubtful because fire doors
   often exceed 20N. If you agree, the "Breach of Code" button should arguably be
   hidden or discouraged on this section.
2. **Edition.** Which NCC edition does the template follow? Its existing references
   (`BCA D3.x`) use the older numbering, but I found D2.21 renumbered to D3D26 in NCC 2022.
   Mixed numbering inside one report would look careless.
3. **Priority of page 3.** I proposed journey priority 4 because it is information rather
   than path of travel. But a missing visual alarm is arguably a barrier to leaving.
   Do you prefer 1?
4. **Scope.** Many premises are not workplaces and have no emergency plan. Should
   page 3 be skipped by the scoping step for those (design brief, Phase 3)?
5. **Dimension in #18.** I used 800 x 1300mm as a figure to check, not one I could confirm
   as the right value. Please give the number you actually apply, or delete the number.
6. **Tone of findings.** For an item such as #16, what do you want the report to say? A
   best-practice gap with no code basis needs different wording from a failure.

## Implementation note (for when this is approved)

Add the three pages at the **end** of `PAGES`, then move them in `seedDefault()` to sit
before the Access Consultant Declaration, the same way Signage is moved. Inserting them
mid-array would renumber every later page and question id and silently break saved
audits. A template already saved in a browser will not pick them up; it needs a
one-off "add LEAVE section" step that gives the new items fresh ids with `genId()`.
